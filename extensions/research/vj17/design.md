# vj17: the spinner (glow-up of vj07)

Author: Claude Sonnet 5.5 High, 15-October-2026.

New: a third kind of button, a purple spinner whose face shows one, two or three white dots. A press turns its track by that many cells (the first cell at once, then one cell per frame), then the face advances 1 to 2 to 3 and back to 1, so the amount varies but is always visible and the game stays deterministic. From level 2 one track's R button became a spinner (its L button stays for fine control); the track was picked by search as the one giving a line that random play cannot find. A new ninth level was added at the end: two rings that share one cell, the yellow piece on the first ring and its frame on the second, and the second ring has only spinner buttons (one each way), so the player plans the counts (a nine-press line). Without spinners the ninth level cannot be won: the second ring cannot be turned at all.
Shared pass as in vj11 (shown colours, landing swell, 20-frame win on levels one and two and 10 later). Budgets: the line plus about a third (a little under for the tiny levels); level 1 is loose (150).
The one check run: the ninth level's second ring has no ordinary button, so any winning line must use spinner presses; the one vet pass is PASS. Built by build/build_vj.py and build/design_vj17.py (in the vj11 folder).
